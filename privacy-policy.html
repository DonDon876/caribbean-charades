Caribbean Charades

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026

The short version
  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.
  • Player names, team names, scores and settings stay on your phone.
  • The reaction camera is off unless you switch it on, and videos never leave your phone unless you share them.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

1. Who we are

Caribbean Charades is made in Jamaica by Sheldon Levene, trading as Realisticworks, Kingston, Jamaica ("we", "us"). For the Data Protection Act, 2020 of Jamaica, the EU and UK GDPR and similar laws, we are responsible for the limited information described here. Contact details are in section 9.

2. What the app keeps, and where

The names you type for players and teams, scores, the decks you pick, which cards you've seen and your settings are saved only on your device, so the game remembers them next time. We never receive them. Deleting the app (or clearing its data) deletes them.

3. Reaction camera

The reaction camera is off by default. If you switch it on, the app asks for permission to use the camera and microphone and films each turn with the front camera so you can watch, save or share the moment. Recording happens entirely on your device; we never receive, upload or store your videos. A video is saved to your gallery only if you tap Save, and shared only if you tap Share and choose an app, whose own privacy policy then applies. Please get everyone's agreement before filming, especially children.

4. Playing on a TV

"Play on TV" shows the scoreboard on a TV connected by cable, screen mirroring or casting. The phone sends the TV the scores, team names and timer; it never sends the card being acted out. Nothing passes through our servers.

5. Ratings and purchases

After a few finished games the app may show Google Play's own rating window. Google handles it under its privacy policy; we only see the public rating and review if you choose to leave one. This version has no ads and nothing to buy.

6. Internet use

The game works offline. It goes online only when you tap a link (for example, Jamaica opens Jamdung - Jamaica Charades in Google Play) or when casting to a TV.

7. Children

Caribbean Charades is a party game for a general audience aged 13 and up, and it isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If a future version adds anything that changes this policy (for example ads or anonymous play statistics), we'll update this page and its effective date first.
